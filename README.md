# ✨ RefineAI: AI Rewriter, Grammar Checker & Paraphraser

**The ultimate AI writing assistant to rewrite sentences, fix grammar, and paraphrase instantly.**

RefineAI is a powerful Chrome extension designed to be your personal editor, ghostwriter, and communication coach—all powered by the world's most advanced AI. Whether you're drafting a professional email, polishing a LinkedIn post, or just making sure your grammar is perfect, RefineAI is there to help.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📝 Smart Writing Tools
*   **Rewrite:** Instantly transform any sentence to make it more professional, engaging, or clear.
*   **Rephrase:** Say the same thing in a completely different way while keeping the original meaning.
*   **Grammar & Spelling:** Beyond a simple spellcheck—RefineAI understands context to fix complex grammatical errors.
*   **Tone Adjustment:** Change your writing style on the fly. Go from "Friendly" for a teammate to "Professional" for a CEO in one click.

### 📧 AI Email Writer
Struggling with a blank screen? Just tell RefineAI what you want to say, and it will draft a complete, polished email for you. From "Requesting a meeting" to "Follow-up after an interview," we've got you covered.

### 💬 Smart Reply & Chat
Right in your browser sidebar! Use our AI Chat to ask questions, summarize long articles, or get instant replies for messages. It understands the page you are looking at to give you relevant context.

### 📜 Writing History
Never lose a great rewrite again. RefineAI securely saves your writing history (tied to your account), so you can go back and find that perfect sentence you wrote yesterday.

## 🛠 How to Use RefineAI

1.  **Select Text:** Highlight any text on any website.
2.  **Right-Click (Optional):** Use the context menu to quickly "Refine" your selection.
3.  **Open the Sidebar:** Click the RefineAI icon in your Chrome toolbar to access the full suite of tools.
4.  **Refine & Copy:** Watch the AI work its magic, then simply copy the result back into your work.

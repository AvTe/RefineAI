Frequently Asked Questions

How do credits work?

Credits are measured in words. Every text you refine, rewrite, or generate consumes words from your daily limit. Your limit resets every day at midnight.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! You can cancel your subscription at any time. You'll continue to have access to premium features until the end of your billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and other local options through our secure payment partner Lemon Squeezy.

Do unused credits carry over?

Daily word limits reset at midnight each day and do not carry over. This ensures fair usage for all users.

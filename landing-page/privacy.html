Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 23, 2026

At RefineAI, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Chrome extension.

1. Overview

RefineAI is a productivity tool designed to help you rewrite and refine text. Our core philosophy is that your data belongs to you. We do not sell your data, and we do not store your personal conversations on our servers.

2. Information We Collect

  • Text Content: When you use RefineAI to rewrite or reply to a message, the selected text is sent to our AI providers (Groq or Google Gemini) for processing. We do not store this text once the request is complete.
  • Settings & Preferences: Your custom persona, target language, and usage stats are stored locally on your device using Chrome's local storage. This data never leaves your computer unless you explicitly interact with the AI.
  • Technical Data: We may collect anonymous, non-identifying technical data (such as version number or extension errors) to help us improve the tool.

3. How We Use Information

We use the text content provided solely to generate the AI responses you request. We do not use your data to train our own models or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption to protect text while it is in transit to our AI providers. Our providers (Groq and Google) have their own strict privacy policies regarding how they handle data processed through their APIs.

5. Third-Party Services

RefineAI integrates with third-party AI models. By using the extension, you agree to the privacy policies of these providers:

  • Groq Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at via our GitHub profile.
